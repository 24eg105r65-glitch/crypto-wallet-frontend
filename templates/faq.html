Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about CryptoWallet's tracking, security, enclaves, and personal finance features.

How does CryptoWallet track my spending and calculate savings?

Every expense, recurring bill, and allowance is categorized in real time. The platform calculates your 50/30/20 budget envelope (Needs, Wants, Savings) and projects your compound growth at 12% p.a., keeping you in control of every rupee.

How are digital asset and cryptocurrency balances secured?

CryptoWallet balances are denominated in both fiat (INR ₹) and digital assets (BTC, ETH, SOL, USDT) with precision arithmetic (8 decimal places) in the relational schema. All transfers are executed atomically to eliminate double-spend risks.

Is my personal and financial data private?

Yes. CryptoWallet complies with DPDP Act 2023 standards. Passwords are never stored in plaintext and are salted with BCrypt. We never sell your transaction history or share telemetry with third-party advertisers.

Can I claim free testnet funds for experimentation?

Yes! Each asset enclave features an instant testnet faucet. Simply visit your wallet details page or use the quick action menu on the Private Reserve dashboard to claim test funds.
