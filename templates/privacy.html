Privacy Policy

Your privacy and data sovereignty are fundamental pillars of the CryptoWallet financial architecture.

1. Information We Collect

We collect account registration data (username, email, salted hashed passwords) and financial tracking inputs to calculate your budgets and expense metrics. We do not sell your personal financial records.

2. Cryptographic Security & Passwords

All passwords and sensitive tokens are hashed using salted BCrypt cryptography prior to persistence. Plaintext credentials never touch disk or logs.

3. Session Control

Active login sessions are monitored for suspicious anomalies and can be terminated at any time from your account profile settings.
