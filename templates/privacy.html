Privacy Policy

Your privacy and cryptographic data integrity are fundamental to CryptoVault's design.

1. Information We Collect

We collect account registration data (username, email, encrypted passwords) and cryptographic wallet addresses to facilitate transactions.

2. Password Protection

All passwords are hashed with strong, salted BCrypt cryptography prior to persistence in MySQL. Plaintext credentials never touch persistent disks.

3. Session Security

Active login sessions are monitored for suspicious IP changes and can be revoked by the user at any time from their security profile.
