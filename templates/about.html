Technical Architecture & Engineering Specs

CryptoWallet Platform Architecture

A disciplined, 5-layer server-rendered fintech and personal money management platform engineered by Adabala Pushkaran (24EG105R65).

1. Five-Layer Separation of Concerns

CryptoWallet strictly isolates architectural concerns so each layer communicates solely with its immediate neighbour, ensuring rock-solid security boundaries and testability.

Layer 1: Presentation (Thymeleaf & Minimal Reactive Islands)
Full server-side rendering for instant First Contentful Paint, search crawler optimization, and zero client hydration penalty for fast financial updates.
Layer 2: Security & Routing (Spring Security 6)
Intercepts requests, enforces session validation, verifies CSRF tokens on all POST requests, and protects sensitive admin endpoints via ROLE_ADMIN.
Layer 3: Controller Layer (Spring MVC & REST)
Validates HTTP request bodies and DTO constraints, controls model bindings, and dispatches business flows directly to transactional services.
Layer 4: Business & Service Layer (@Transactional)
Executes money logic: atomic debit-credit balance mutations, fee calculations, budget thresholds, and event notifications.
Layer 5: Data Access & Relational Persistence (Spring Data JPA + MySQL)
Normalized schema mapping 6 entities (User, Wallet, Currency, Transaction, AuthSession, Notification) with ACID consistency guarantees.
